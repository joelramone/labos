# Recruitment Plan — First Wave

| Campo | Valor |
|---|---|
| Planned interviews | 10–15 qualitative interviews |
| Objective | Maximum behavioral variation and emerging-pattern discovery |
| Statistical representativeness | Not required and not claimed |
| Outreach status | NOT STARTED |
| Data policy | Minimum participant information; no video, images or medical history |

## Inclusion criteria

- Approximately 40–55 years old as an initial recruitment boundary.
- Currently training, recently trained, or made a concrete attempt to start/return within the recent past.
- Can describe actual behavior and alternatives rather than only abstract preferences.
- Can provide informed consent for notes.

## Exclusion criteria

- Participation sought primarily for clinical diagnosis, treatment or rehabilitation advice.
- Inability to consent.
- Expectation of workout prescription or movement correction during the interview.
- Need to collect sensitive evidence outside the approved Phase 1 policy.

## Candidate recruitment channels

[Hipótesis operativa] Personal network referrals, local gyms/clubs, training communities, professional networks and participant referrals may provide variation. No channel is authorized for outreach by this document alone.

## Screening questions

1. ¿Entrenás actualmente o intentaste comenzar/retomar recientemente?
2. ¿Cuál describe mejor tu contexto: home, gym, club u otro?
3. ¿Te considerás beginner, returning, consistently active o experienced? Permitir respuesta propia.
4. ¿Qué tipo de entrenamiento ocupa la mayor parte de tu práctica?
5. ¿Aceptás una conversación de 35–50 minutos con notas anonimizadas y sin video?

[Decisión] No preguntar diagnósticos, lesiones, peso, body composition, employer, income u otros datos innecesarios para screening.

## Scheduling and records

- Assign participant ID before interview.
- Store contact details separately from research notes and delete when follow-up ends.
- Offer time slots compatible with the 3–5 h/week allocation.
- Do not record audio/video unless a future protocol is separately approved; default is researcher notes only.

## Variation plan

Seek coverage across:

- returning and consistently active;
- home and gym/club;
- beginner and experienced;
- strength/skill-focused and general fitness;
- time-constrained contexts as a cross-cutting dimension.

## Saturation trigger

[Decisión] Stop the first wave when either:

- 10+ interviews have been completed and two consecutive interviews add no materially new problem pattern, workaround, consequence or contradiction; or
- 15 interviews are completed.

If a narrow segment continues producing new strong evidence, classify `NARROW` and design a focused follow-up wave rather than expanding Phase 1 indiscriminately.

## Ethical boundaries

- Participation voluntary; withdrawal permitted.
- No diagnosis, coaching or corrective advice.
- No deceptive feature pitch.
- No unnecessary personal data.
- Summarize quotes; avoid excessive verbatim transcription.
- Contact no one during preparation of this plan.
