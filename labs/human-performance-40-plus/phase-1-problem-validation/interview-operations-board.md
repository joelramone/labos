# Interview Operations Board — First Wave

> Capacity: up to 15 interviews. Use Participant IDs only; never enter real names.

## Allowed participant statuses

`NOT_IDENTIFIED`, `IDENTIFIED`, `CONTACTED`, `RESPONDED`, `SCREENED`, `SCHEDULED`, `COMPLETED`, `DECLINED`, `NO_RESPONSE`, `EXCLUDED`

## Field conventions

- `Contact status`: one allowed participant status.
- `Screening status`: `NOT_IDENTIFIED`, `IDENTIFIED`, `SCREENED`, or `EXCLUDED`.
- `Interview status`: `NOT_IDENTIFIED`, `SCHEDULED`, `COMPLETED`, `DECLINED`, `NO_RESPONSE`, or `EXCLUDED`.
- Boolean completion fields: `NO`, `YES`, or `N/A` only.
- `Data sensitivity check`: `PENDING`, `PASS`, or `ESCALATE`.
- Scheduled date: ISO 8601 or `—`.
