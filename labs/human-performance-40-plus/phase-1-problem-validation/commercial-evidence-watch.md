# Commercial Evidence Watch

## Evidence classes

| Class | Definition | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| `BEHAVIORAL_EVIDENCE` | Actual spending, renewal, cancellation, paid trial, switching, active search or meaningful effort | Highest |
| `STATED_INTENT` | Participant says they might spend time or money without prior behavior | Secondary only |
| `NO_EVIDENCE` | No relevant commercial behavior or intent captured | None |

## Capture rules

- Record current or past behavior before stated intent.
- Spending counts only when linked to a relevant problem or job.
- Equipment spending is included only when purchased because of a specific problem.
- Do not infer willingness-to-pay from gym membership alone; document what job it serves.
- Do not define or test pricing in Phase 1.
- Preserve cancellations and refusal to spend as contradictory evidence.
