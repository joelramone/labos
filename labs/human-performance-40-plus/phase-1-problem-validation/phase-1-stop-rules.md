# Phase 1 Stop Rules

## `STOP_EARLY`

Apply when sufficient behavioral variation has been sampled and:

- the target group consistently reports no meaningful recurrent problem;
- evidence is dominated by hypothetical interest or prompted enthusiasm;
- no recurring problem appears after adequate variation;
- current alternatives resolve the relevant problems with low dissatisfaction;
- privacy, safety or capacity constraints prevent responsible continuation.

[Decisión] `STOP_EARLY` produces a documented recommendation; closing or pausing Phase 1 remains a Founder decision.

## `NARROW`

Apply when a strong problem exists only in a specific behavioral segment or context, while evidence elsewhere is weak or contradicted.

Required evidence: concrete recurrence + consequence + workaround/search/switching/spending concentrated in that segment.

## `CONTINUE`

Apply when evidence shows:

- a recurring problem;
- meaningful consequence;
- an existing workaround or active search;
- behavioral effort, switching or spending;
- no unresolved safety or evidence-integrity blocker.

## `PIVOT`

Apply when the initial problem is weak but a different recurring, consequential problem emerges with stronger behavior.

## Escalation boundary

`ESCALATE_TO_SOLUTION_VALIDATION` only after an explicit Founder decision following Phase 1 synthesis. It does not authorize an app, Movement Analysis, software, pricing tests or Phase 2 execution automatically.

## Evidence rules

- “I like it” and “I would use it” do not satisfy any continue rule.
- Interview count alone does not justify continuation.
- Contradictions can cause narrowing, pivoting or stopping.
- Movement Analysis remains a dormant hypothesis even if a feedback problem emerges; solution feasibility is a later question.
