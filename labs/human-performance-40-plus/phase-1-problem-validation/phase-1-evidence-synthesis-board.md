## Synthesis discipline

- Supporting and contradicting interviews use Participant IDs, not names.
- Recurrence and severity remain qualitative.
- Do not add solution ideas to cluster evidence; record them separately as hypotheses.
- “Recovery” reflects participant perception only and cannot become a clinical claim.
- A cluster cannot advance solely from researcher interest or a pitched response.
