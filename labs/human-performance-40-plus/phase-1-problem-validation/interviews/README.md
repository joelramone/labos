# Phase 1 Interview Records

## Purpose

This directory stores completed interview-session records and evidence notes using Participant IDs only. It currently contains no participant records.

## Naming convention

- Session record: `HP40-P-NNN-session.md`
- Optional follow-up record: `HP40-P-NNN-followup-YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- Participant ID range for first wave: `HP40-P-001` through `HP40-P-015`
- Evidence ID: `HP40-P1-EV-NNN`

## What may be stored

- Segment hypothesis and training context.
- Concise notes about current/past behavior.
- Problems, frequency, consequences and workarounds.
- Existing alternatives, spending categories and switching behavior.
- Short quote summaries.
- Contradictory evidence and researcher interpretation, clearly separated.
- Scorecard result and affected hypothesis IDs.

## What must not be stored

- Real names or unnecessary contact details.
- Medical history, diagnoses or detailed injury records.
- Body images, training video or audio recordings.
- Exact addresses, employer, income, identity documents or unrelated personal data.
- Clinical interpretation or movement-correction claims.
- Excessive verbatim transcripts.

## Privacy rules

- Contact information, if required for scheduling, is stored separately and deleted after follow-up closes.
- Participants may withdraw; remove non-required notes according to the approved policy.
- Volunteered sensitive comments are minimized and not clinically interpreted.
- Any need for video, images, health data or recordings requires a new approved protocol.

## Relationship with synthesis

Interview records are source evidence. Synthesis boards reference Participant/Evidence IDs and must preserve supporting and contradictory evidence. The synthesis board does not replace source notes, and interpretations never become `[Hecho]` without appropriate evidence.
