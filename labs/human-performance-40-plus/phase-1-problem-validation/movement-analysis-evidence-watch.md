# Movement Analysis Evidence Watch

## Purpose

[Decisión] Track evidence relevant to Movement Analysis without introducing the solution artificially. This board does not validate Movement Analysis.

## Signal types

- `SPONTANEOUS_SIGNAL`: participant raises the topic before any neutral technical-feedback question or solution cue. Greater weight for problem discovery.
- `PROMPTED_SIGNAL`: topic emerges after a neutral approved question about execution or feedback. Useful, but lower discovery weight.

## Eligible signals

- Uncertainty about exercise execution.
- Need for technical feedback.
- Recording oneself or asking others for opinions.
- Paying for coaching to resolve technical uncertainty.
- Comparing videos or attempts.
- Fear or technical doubt without clinical interpretation.
- Difficulty self-correcting or identifying improvement.
- Desire to validate what was performed successfully.

## Contradictory evidence

Record when participants do not experience the problem, resolve it easily, distrust video feedback, do not value technical feedback, or prefer direct human coaching.

## Bias controls

- Never mention Movement Analysis as a product during Phase 1.
- Do not convert curiosity about video into problem evidence.
- Do not infer correction feasibility from problem demand.
- Keep status `DORMANT / FUTURE HYPOTHESIS` until later gates.
