# Phase 1 First-Wave Execution Pack

> Minimal sequence for the Founder. No app pitch, video, medical questions or solution validation.

## Ten-step loop

1. **Identify 5 candidates** — assign `HP40-P-001` to `005`; enter segment hypothesis and source in [interview-operations-board.md](./interview-operations-board.md).
2. **Screen** — use [participant-screening-script.md](./participant-screening-script.md); mark `SCREENED`, `EXCLUDED` or `DECLINED`.
3. **Recruit** — manually choose the appropriate text from [recruitment-message-templates.md](./recruitment-message-templates.md). Do not automate outreach.
4. **Schedule** — record ISO date and mark `SCHEDULED`; store contact details outside research notes.
5. **Interview** — use [interview-guide.md](./interview-guide.md) for 20–30 minutes; do not pitch hypotheses.
6. **Capture evidence** — complete one copy of [interview-session-template.md](./interview-session-template.md) and the approved evidence template under `interviews/`.
7. **Complete scorecard** — assess qualitative problem signals in [problem-evidence-scorecard.md](./problem-evidence-scorecard.md).
8. **Update hypothesis matrix** — add evidence IDs and contradictions to [hypothesis-evidence-matrix.md](./hypothesis-evidence-matrix.md).
9. **Update synthesis** — update [phase-1-evidence-synthesis-board.md](./phase-1-evidence-synthesis-board.md), plus Movement/commercial watches when applicable.
10. **Choose next segment** — recruit the contrast that most reduces uncertainty; check [interview-quality-checklist.md](./interview-quality-checklist.md) and saturation.

## Before the first interview

- [ ] Participant is screened and voluntarily scheduled.
- [ ] Participant ID assigned; no real name in research files.
- [ ] Notes-only consent language ready.
- [ ] No video/audio/body image collection.
- [ ] Interview guide and session template open.
- [ ] Researcher remembers: behavior before opinion; contradiction before confirmation.

## Immediately after

- Complete evidence, scorecard and synthesis before the next interview.
- Label Movement signals `SPONTANEOUS_SIGNAL` or `PROMPTED_SIGNAL`.
- Label commercial material `BEHAVIORAL_EVIDENCE`, `STATED_INTENT` or `NO_EVIDENCE`.
- If sensitive information appears, minimize and escalate rather than interpreting it.
