# Phase 1 Weekly Cadence — 3–5 Hours/Week

[Decisión] The cadence is a capacity envelope, not an interview quota. Stop when saturation or a stop rule is reached.

Initial target: 10–15 qualitative interviews, subject to earlier saturation.

## Week 1 — Start and calibrate

- 30–45 min: identify up to 5 candidates and update operations board.
- 30–45 min: screen/recruit only approved candidates.
- 60–90 min: conduct 2–3 interviews of 20–30 minutes.
- 60–90 min: capture evidence, score and synthesize immediately.
- 15 min: select next recruitment contrast.

## Week 2 — Increase variation

- Recruit missing behavioral segments.
- Conduct 3–4 interviews if capacity permits.
- Update clusters after every interview.
- Review contradictions and adjust the next segment, not the interview questions.

## Week 3 — Contradictory sampling

- Conduct 3–4 interviews across segments likely to contradict emerging patterns.
- Compare returning/active, home/gym and beginner/experienced contexts.
- Check saturation and preliminary `CONTINUE/NARROW/PIVOT/STOP` evidence.

## Week 4 — Close only if needed

- Conduct remaining interviews only if saturation has not occurred.
- Do not force 15 interviews.
- Complete synthesis, scorecards and hypothesis matrix.
- Run Phase 1 decision gate; escalation requires Founder decision.

## Saturation indicators

Saturation may be declared after at least 10 completed interviews when two consecutive interviews across relevant variation add no materially new:

- problem cluster;
- consequence;
- workaround;
- switching or spending behavior;
- material contradiction;
- segment-specific distinction.

## Capacity protection

- Maximum operational target: 3–5 hours/week.
- If documentation falls behind, pause new interviews before accumulating evidence debt.
- If fewer interviews fit, preserve immediate synthesis rather than increasing volume.
- A missed week does not justify compressing unsafe or leading interviews later.
