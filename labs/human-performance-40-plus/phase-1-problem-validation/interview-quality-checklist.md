## Failure handling

- A leading or pitched response is not discarded, but labeled `PROMPTED_SIGNAL` or downgraded in evidence strength.
- Sensitive data captured accidentally is minimized or removed and the event is escalated.
- An interview with incomplete evidence remains `COMPLETED` as a session but cannot support a decision gate until remediation or limitation is documented.
